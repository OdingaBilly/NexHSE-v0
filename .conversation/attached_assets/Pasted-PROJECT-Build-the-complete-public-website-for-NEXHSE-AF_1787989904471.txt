PROJECT

Build the complete public website for:

NEXHSE AFRICA

Domain: https://nexhse.co.ke

Business: Workplace Safety & Professional Development

NexHSE is a workplace health, safety, environmental and professional-development company serving organisations across Africa.

The website must feel like a modern professional HSE institution, not a generic corporate template, government portal, training-centre brochure or fire-equipment ecommerce site.

The design should communicate:

Trust.
Technical competence.
Human protection.
Professionalism.
Modern safety culture.
Operational practicality.
Regulatory seriousness.
Continuous development.

The experience should feel sufficiently premium that an operations director, HR manager, factory manager, school administrator, construction executive or corporate decision-maker immediately feels:

“These are people I would trust with workplace safety.”

Use the blueprint's typographic direction:

Headings

Cambria or a refined serif with a similar authoritative technical-document character.

Body

Calibri or a clean modern sans-serif equivalent.

However, the final implementation should feel contemporary rather than like an office document.

Use typography to create contrast between:

institutional authority

modern digital clarity.
